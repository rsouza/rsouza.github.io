ÖAW · NLP · 2024–Present
Detecting Biases in the Austrian Media Corpus
Austrian Academy of Sciences / ACDH-CH — 2024–Present
Project coordinator (with Amelie Dorn) using the Austrian Media Corpus (APA-AMC) — one of the largest German-language text corpora, with ~50 million articles and 12.4 billion tokens spanning decades of Austrian print media — to detect and analyse linguistic and topical biases embedded in the corpus. Combines NLP, word embeddings, and bias detection methodologies to produce insights into media representation and language change in Austria.
FGV EMAp · FGV IBRE
Web-scraped Inflation Index (IPC-W)
Principal Investigator — 2015–2019
Co-directed research calculating consumer price indexes derived from web-scraped retail prices. Built robust scraping pipelines and statistical modeling scripts (based on Cavallo's Billion Prices Project methodology) to provide daily geospatial inflation indicators for Rio de Janeiro.
FGV EMAp · Open Source
PyPLN — Distributed NLP Pipeline
Principal Investigator — 2011–2016
Led the design and development of PyPLN, an open-source, high-performance distributed NLP pipeline built in Python, Celery, and MongoDB. Served as the core analytical engine for multiple FGV research projects, including the Supreme Court in Numbers. Described in: PyPLN: A Distributed Platform for Natural Language Processing (F. C. Coelho, R. R. Souza et al., 2013).
FGV · Legal Informatics
The Supreme Court in Numbers
Principal Investigator / Researcher — 2011–2018
Led data modeling and text mining of millions of judicial decisions from the Brazilian Supreme Court (STF). Uncovered structural patterns in judicial votes, mapped lawyer–client networks, and produced empirical findings adopted by legal scholars and cited in Brazilian judicial reform debates. Key publications: Information Mining and Visualization of STF Data (2012) · The Consumer Litigation Industry (Social Networks, Elsevier, 2015).
